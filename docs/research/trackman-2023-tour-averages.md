# TrackMan 2023 Tour Averages — transcription

Source: https://www.trackman.com/blog/introducing-updated-tour-averages
(table images: https://a.storyblok.com/f/117513/1920x883/6aefae601d/pga_tour-averages_trackman_blog.jpg
 and https://a.storyblok.com/f/117513/1920x883/9617e89ecd/lpga_tour-averages_trackman_blog.jpg).
Transcribed 2026-10-08 and verified against 1.5x crops of both images.
The article states no measurement conditions (altitude, temperature); the
project assumes the published values describe flight at sea level.

Columns: club speed (mph), attack angle (deg), ball speed (mph), smash factor,
launch angle (deg), spin rate (rpm), max height (yd/m), land angle (deg), carry (yd/m).

Meters are NOT always round(yards*0.9144): PGA 4 Iron is 209 yd / 192 m
(209*0.9144 = 191.1), so TrackMan rounded both units from unrounded values.
